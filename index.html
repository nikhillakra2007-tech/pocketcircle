The Fractured Rupee of Young India

Between messy WhatsApp bill-splitting groups and aggressive Buy-Now-Pay-Later ads, students and first-jobbers leak wealth before it begins.

Phase 01 · 18 to 22 Years

The Campus Friction Trap

Room rent, groceries, and late-night canteen runs tracked informally cause uncollected debts and social hesitation. Without proactive warnings, pocket money vanishes by the 18th of the month.

  • Awkward Dues Unsettled UPI requests trapped in WhatsApp chat history.
  • Impulse Blindness No visibility into how tonight's party affects next week's mess dues.
  • Zero Cushion 86% of college students hold under ₹5,000 in emergency backup.
Phase 02 · 23 to 35 Years

The First-Salary Illusion

The first paycheck arrives, followed immediately by gadget EMIs, security deposits, and lifestyle creep. Traditional banking apps display raw balance without mapping it to actual life goals.

  • Hidden Liabilities Gadget BNPL and student loan outflows never shown on one screen.
  • Idle Capital Loss Hard-earned savings rot in 2.7% accounts losing value to real inflation.
  • Advisory Void Wealth apps bombard with stock tips; nobody guides on life timelines.
