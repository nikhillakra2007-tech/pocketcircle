05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about splits, UPI settlements, and security.

How does the 1-Tap UPI split settlement work? +

PocketCircle generates a dynamic UPI intent pre-loaded with the exact debt amount and recipient UPI ID. Tapping settle opens GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm with the amount pre-filled—eliminating manual VPA typing and screenshot confirmations.

What is the Automatic Goal Sweep? +

Whenever a roommate repays an expense you covered, that money is automatically routed towards your active Goal Vault (like an M3 MacBook, semester trip, or emergency fund) rather than sitting as idle balance in a wallet.

Do my roommates need to download the app to pay? +

No. You can share a quick PocketCircle settlement link via WhatsApp. Your flatmates can scan the dynamic UPI QR or tap the intent link to settle directly from their favorite UPI app without having an account.

Is my financial data secure under the RBI framework? +

Yes. PocketCircle integrates with RBI-regulated Account Aggregators (AA). All access is read-only, 256-bit encrypted, and can only be enabled with your explicit cryptographic consent.

How is PocketCircle different from Splitwise or INDmoney? +

Splitwise only tracks debts without budgeting or wealth building. Traditional wealth apps are designed for salaried professionals. PocketCircle bridges campus life and early career: unifying roommate splits, daily runway protection, and micro-savings into one calm ledger.
